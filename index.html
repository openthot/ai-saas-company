Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code to use ApexSaaS? ↓
Not at all! You simply describe what you want in plain English. The AI creates the interface, buttons, forms, and connects the cloud database automatically.
Is the cloud database really free? ↓
Yes! Every deployed app gets connected to a free Supabase PostgreSQL cloud instance with 500MB of storage and SSL security included at zero cost.
Why do I need to create an account before deploying? ↓
Your account links your deployed app to your private cloud database so that only you can access your customer entries, update your settings, or manage your live domain.
Can I use my own custom domain? ↓
Yes, with the Pro Creator plan you can attach any custom domain (e.g. www.yourbusiness.com) with automatic HTTPS certificates provided.
